Shading and style review: the old drawing's own shapes, from your characters

{{intro}}

1 · The shapes are the old drawing's

Every shape the old portrait engine draws for a character is converted path by path into mannequin points (with their handles), in the same paint order. Nothing is redrawn or approximated.

2 · Shading is shapes on top

The old drawing lays shadow shapes over a flat base: a plane down one side of the face, the inside of an ear, the nose, the lid, the fold of a top. The mannequin keeps them as shading layers.

3 · One tone rule

A shadow is the old shade(colour, amount): darker, a little richer, the hue nudged toward red, at the shape's own opacity. There are no highlights in the old drawing. The light comes from the upper left.

4 · What the review shows

For each character: the old picture, the same shapes drawn from the converted points, the base shapes alone, and a map of where the shading shapes lie. Under them, the tone steps the old drawing uses on this skin and hair.
