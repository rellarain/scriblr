# Character models

Character models are the shapes characters will be drawn from: the body, the clothes and the hair, arranged in **groups** of **objects** made of **shapes**. They are **designed in the Admin console** (Configuration > Characters) and saved as drafts and published one by one, the way the Plot presets are. The designer draws shapes, arranges them, and (since the second phase) **rigs** them: groups nest and turn about pivots, are fixed to a shape of their parent, and are driven by **sliders**. It also **pairs** left and right (a twin that mirrors another shape, object or group), **links points** (clothes that stay on the body, and loosen with a slider) and shows the **back view** with outlines of their own. Since the third phase an object can have **forms** (alternate outlines along axes such as the shape of an eye), the model keeps named **presets** (poses, expressions, ages, builds, outfits), objects and groups can be **switched off** with an eye to look at the rest, and the figure can be **posed by dragging**. It does not yet give shapes colours or classes (later phases), and it stores nothing about any character.

## The page

`.cmSplit` holds the list (`.cmList`) and the open model (`.cmPane`). The page keeps the Admin's accent. Below 860px of its own width the list folds into one picker button (`.cmPick`) above the model, and the tree, the canvas and the inspector stack; below 1180px the inspector drops under the canvas.

- **List**: one row per model, its name and a **status badge** (`.cmBadge`): *Published v3*, *v3 · unpublished changes*, *Unpublished (was v2)*, *Not published*. Each state has a shape as well as a colour (a tick, a filled dot, a hollow dot, a grey dot). Rows reorder by their grip or Alt + Arrow.
- **Tools** (`.cmTools`): the model's name, its status, then **Publish**, **Unpublish**, **Discard changes**, **Versions** (the published version and the ten before it, each with *Bring into draft*), **Duplicate** and **Delete** (which asks in place first). Publish stays disabled, and the Check panel says why, while the draft is not valid.
- **Tree** (`.cmTree`) with a **Layers | Rig** switch (`.cmTreeModes`, two icon buttons with `aria-pressed`). *Layers*: three layers, **Body, Clothing and Hair**; under each, the groups that have an object on it, their objects and the objects' shapes. A row selects what it names; the buttons add a group, an object (+ beside a layer) or a shape (a template menu beside an object), copy and delete. Groups, objects and shapes **reorder by their grip** (`.cmGrip`; drag, or Alt + Up / Down), and a shape's up and down arrows do the same one step at a time; the order in the tree is the **draw order** (a shape lower in the list is drawn in front, unless its `z` says otherwise). *Rig* (`.cmRigTree`): the groups nested under the group they hang from, each with its level in the pivot chain (`.cmCount`, bold at six, the most); **drag a group's grip onto another to hang it there**, onto the empty space to unhang it, or press **Alt + Right / Left** on the grip to indent or outdent it. A move that would make a circle or a chain longer than six is refused, with the reason beside the canvas. It has a search. Counts beside groups and objects turn bold with a bar under them above the warning limit. Every group and object row has an **eye** (`aria-pressed`, pressed while it is shown): see *Show and hide* below.
- **Canvas** (`.cmCanvas`, an SVG): the shapes in a neutral token (the selection in the accent, the rest dimmed), a grid, the **mirror line** at the centre, and, for the selected shape in the point editor, its **points** and the **curve handles** of the selected point. The shapes are drawn **where the rig puts them**: a shape in a turned group is drawn turned, its points and handles drawn through the group's matrix and a drag undone through it, so a shape can be edited with a slider anywhere on its range. A shape whose points a slider moves, with that slider away from rest, can only be looked at (a note says which, and the toolbar's reset puts the sliders back). The selected group shows its **pivot** (`.cmPivot`: a cross in a ring, drag it or focus it and press the arrow keys; Shift for bigger steps; for a group fixed to a shape the arrow keys step the anchor: to the next or previous point, along the edge, or across and down the shape) and, if it is fixed to a shape, the **anchor** (`.cmAnchor`, a diamond). The mirror line is drawn at the model's own axis. A **twin** is drawn with a dashed outline and its points dimmed: it is shown, not edited. A **linked** point (`.cmPoint--linked`) has a heavier ring in the accent and is not dragged: the arrow keys adjust its link instead (left and right slide it along the edge or change a follow's offset across, up and down change how far out it sits or the offset down; Shift: five times as much). Left button: select a shape, drag a point or a handle, or (in move mode) the whole shape. Double-click an edge: add a point there. **Right button: a click on the grid unselects everything and shows the end result; a click on a shape moves through point editor, move shape and unselected; a drag pans.** Arrow keys move the selected point (Shift: bigger steps; in move mode the whole shape), Delete removes the point, Escape unselects.
- **Toolbar** (`.cmToolbar`, icon buttons with a label and a title): undo, redo, point editor, move shape, **Pose mode** (the hand; see below), mirror editing, snap, grid, zoom in and out, fit the whole figure, **Front view** and **Back view** (two icon buttons with `aria-pressed`; the back view turns the figure round about the mirror line), new shape from a template, and the inspector drawer. There are **no typed number fields**: points are moved by dragging and by the arrow keys.
- **Inspector** (`.cmInspector`, a drawer the toolbar folds away) with five tabs (`role="tablist"`, icon and label, Left and Right arrows move between them, the strip wraps when it is narrow): **Shape**, **Rig**, **Ranges**, **Forms** and **Presets** (below). With nothing selected the Shape tab shows the model's name, description and the **Mirror line** (a stepper: the x that mirror editing reflects about, 40 to 160, 100 to begin with). Shape tab: the name, and for an object its layer; for a shape its view (both, front only, back only), its opacity, its template, and its points as numbered chips with the four **point modes** (curve, sharp, curve then sharp, sharp then curve), add a point after the selected one, delete it, reset its handles. Below the points, for a shape: **Pair**, **Links** and **In the front / back view** (all described below). For an object, Pair too; for a group, Pair is in the Rig tab.
- **Previews** (`.cmStrip`, always on, in the view being edited): the selection, or with nothing selected the whole model, at random sizes, tilts and backgrounds, **with every slider at a random value inside its range** (the slider's `rand` range when it has one); *Random* with a re-roll, *Smallest* and *Largest* as stress previews (Smallest also puts every slider at its minimum, Largest at its maximum), and three sizes. The tiles pair a shape token with a ground token so each is readable on all four backgrounds.
- **Checks** (`.cmCheck`): the size of a character's copy of the model, what stops Publish, which limits the model is at or past, and the **rig check** (a slider that drives nothing and slides no anchor and loosens no shape, an anchor that does not keep its group where it is drawn at rest, a shape that leaves the frame when a slider is at its minimum or maximum; at most eight lines), the **notes about forms** (a form that changes a shape which has an outline of its own for the back shows from the front only; a form with another number of points than the base is followed by anchors, links and sliders by the base outline) and the **forms-by-views grid** (`.cmGrid`, a table with a row for each form: what it does from the front, and what becomes of it from behind).
- **Model files**: the model's toolbar has an **Export** icon (the open model's draft) and the list has **Import** and **Export all**. A file (`scriblrCharacterModels`, version 1) holds drafts; an import adds each as an **unpublished draft** (a name that is taken becomes *Name (copy)*), skips what is not a valid model and says what it skipped in a notice (`.cmToast`) with a Dismiss button; a file that is not a model file is refused with an alert.

## Pairs, links and the back view

- **Pair** (`PairPanel`): *Make a twin* gives a shape, an object (with its shapes) or a group (with everything in it and under it) a **twin**: a derived mirror image about the mirror line, with no points of its own, that follows every edit of the original and every slider that moves it. A twin is marked in the tree (`.cmTwin`, the swap icon) and named *Name (twin)*. Its outline is the reflection of the original's, in the group's own space, so a pair of eyes in one group tilt with the head. A twin **group** does what its original does, mirrored, from its own parent: with the same parent for both (the torso) a lean of the torso carries both arms alike; with twin parents (left arm and forearm) it is the plain reflection. A twin has no pivot, anchor, template or point list of its own, and a slider may not drive it (use the original). *Break the pair* makes it a shape, object or group of its own, drawn as it was a moment ago: its sliders are copied mirrored (a turn the other way, recorded points moved the other way across, a bend the other way), its anchor is mirrored onto the twin of the original's shape, and its point links are not copied. Breaking a twin group breaks the twin groups under it too. Deleting an original deletes its twin ("Its twin went with it").
- **Links** (`LinksPanel`): select a point and choose **Weld** (exactly on a point of another shape), **Follow** (stay where it is, move with a point of another shape) or **Lock to an edge** (keep its place along the edge of another shape, as far out as it is now), then click the shape on the canvas (the point or the edge nearest the click; Escape stops). The link is kept on the point that follows and is solved when the model is drawn, so the clothes follow the body whatever turns it, across groups. The list shows each link, with steppers for the distance from the edge and the spot along it (or the offset of a follow) and *Unlink* (the point stays where it is drawn). A shape cannot be linked to itself, to a circle of shapes, or from a twin; a model may have 200 links (a warning above 100). Adding or deleting a point of a shape cuts the links to it, with a note.
- **Looseness**: a shape can take its looseness from a slider (*Make a slider* makes one, 0 to 200%, resting at 100%): the distance of its links (outward from an edge, or a follow's offset) is stretched by the slider over its rest value, so 200% drapes the shirt twice as far from the body and 0% pulls it tight.
- **The back view**: the toolbar's *Back view* turns the figure round about the mirror line. Anchors, links and sliders are the same in both views (they read the front outline, so one edit moves both). A shape shows its front outline from behind until its points are edited in the back view; it then has an **outline of its own for the back** (*Use the front outline again* undoes that). Its depth is its own in the back view once it is moved there: *In front* and *Behind* (in the panel *In the front / back view*) move it one place among the shapes shown in the view being edited, and give that view its own depths (the back follows the front until then). Recording points is done in the front view.

## The rig

The **Rig tab** is for the selected group (or the group an object or shape is in):

- **Pivot and parent**: the chain from the root as a breadcrumb (each parent a link), *Level n of 6 in the pivot chain*, a **Hangs from** select (every group that is not below this one), the pivot's place, and a button that puts it in the middle of the group. Everything in the group turns and scales about the pivot.
- **Attached to a shape**: a group with a parent can be fixed to one shape of the parent or of a group above it, by one of four **anchors** (chips, `aria-pressed`): *Point* (one point of the shape; a stepper), *Edge spot* (a spot along one edge; steppers and a slider), *Slides* (along one edge, where a **slider** puts it), *Inside* (a relative place across and down the shape). **Pick on the canvas** lets a click on a shape of the parent choose it. Attaching puts the pivot on the anchor without moving the drawing. A parent move drags the group along with the anchor.
- **Motion**: the sliders that turn, scale or slide this group; each is a link that opens it in the Ranges tab.

## Sliders

The **Ranges tab** lists the model's sliders (`.cmSlider`), by default those that have to do with the selection (*This selection | All*). **New slider** offers four kinds, each a menu item that says why it is unavailable (*Select a group first*):

- **Turn the group**: turns the selected group about its pivot, −20° at the minimum and +20° at the maximum to begin with.
- **Scale**: scales the selected shape or group (80% and 120%), or the whole model about its feet when nothing is selected (*Size*); the chips say whether across, up and down, or both.
- **Move points (record)**: the canvas edits a copy of the selected shape with every slider at rest; **Set maximum** (or *Set minimum*) writes how far each point moved, **Cancel** drops it. Adding or deleting a point is not recorded.
- **Bend the shape**: a smooth bend along a length of the shape (two steppers for where it starts and ends).

A slider has a **Try it** range (the test value is only for looking and is never saved; a *n off rest* button in the toolbar and a *Reset test values* button put them back), a **sweep** button (minimum to maximum and back, still running with reduced motion because it was asked for), steppers for its **minimum, rest and maximum** (no typed numbers), a **show to writers** toggle (an eye) and a delete that asks in place. Under them, what it drives, one row per **drive** with its own steppers for the amount at each end, and **Add what it drives**. A drive's amount is zero (or 100% for scale) at rest, the stated amount at the maximum and the other at the minimum, in straight lines between.

## Forms

The **Forms tab** is for an object (the selected object, or the object of the selected shape; with a group selected it offers the group's objects). An object has **axes** (`.cmAxis`: the shape of an eye, an expression, a style), each with **forms** (`.cmFormCard`, buttons with `aria-pressed`, each with a small picture of the object in that form and *· default* on the one shown unless another is chosen). A form gives some of the object's shapes **an outline of their own**; every other shape keeps its base outline, and an empty outline hides the shape in that form. An object shows one form of every axis; when two axes give the same shape an outline, **the first axis in the list wins** (the arrows reorder axes).

- **Choosing** a form is only for looking, like the Try it sliders: it is not saved, it counts in the toolbar's *n off rest*, and *Reset test values* puts every axis back on its default form.
- **Editing**: while a form is chosen the canvas edits **that form's outline**, not the base outline: a banner in the tab and the line under the canvas say *Editing form Wide of Eye shape*, and moving a point of a shape the form does not change gives the shape an outline of its own in the form. The base outline (what anchors, links, recorded moves and the rig always read) is left alone, so a form may have another number of points than the base. Choose the default form to edit the base outline again. A shape the form hides cannot be edited (the line under the canvas says so); *Use the base outline* brings it back.
- **Add a form from the current one** copies the outlines drawn now that differ from the base; *Rename*, *Make default*, *Move earlier / later* and *Delete* (asks in place; an axis keeps at least one form) are under the chosen form, with a list of what it does to each shape (*own outline*, *base outline*, *hidden in this form*) and the buttons *Hide … in this form* and *Use the base outline for …*. *Add an axis* starts one with a single form, *Standard*.
- A **twin** follows the form its original shows (use the original's tab); in the back view a shape with an outline of its own for the back shows that one whatever the form.

## Presets

A preset is a named set of **slider values** and of **objects and groups switched off** (the *Presets tab*, `.cmPreset`). It does **not** keep which form is chosen. Its **kind** is a free name (Pose, Expression, Age, Build, Outfit, …) and is its slot: **applying a preset puts back whatever any other preset of the same kind controls, then sets its own**, so a pose replaces the last pose and an expression leaves the arms alone. A preset whose sliders and hidden things are what the figure shows now is marked **On** (a tick and the word, within a hundredth of a slider's unit).

- **Save the current as a preset** takes a name and a kind (the kinds in use are suggested) and keeps the sliders that are away from rest and the things switched off. Each row has **Apply**, *Update from the current*, a name field and a delete that asks in place; chips filter by kind.
- A preset that names a slider or a thing that is gone is refused at Publish; deleting a slider or a thing removes it from the presets.

## Show and hide

The **eye** on every group and object row (`aria-pressed`: pressed while shown) switches it off for looking: its shapes are not drawn on the canvas or in the previews, the row is dimmed, and it still does its part in the rig (anchors and links read it). Switching a group off hides the groups hung under it. Like the Try it sliders it is not saved with the model; a preset keeps it. It counts in *n off rest*.

## Pose mode

The hand in the toolbar (**Pose mode**, `aria-pressed`) lets the figure be grabbed instead of edited. Every drag is the inverse of what a slider does, so the sliders follow (watch the Ranges tab) and the result is only test values; *Save as preset* in the **pose bar** (`.cmPoseBar`) keeps a pose as a preset of kind Pose (without the hidden things). Nothing here changes the model.

- **Drag a part** to turn its group about the pivot: the slider whose Turn drive turns that group (or the nearest group above it that has one) is set from the angle the pointer sweeps, held to its range. A ring (`.cmJoint`) marks each pivot that can turn. A twin cannot be posed (pose its original).
- **Drag a square** (`.cmScaleHandle`) on a group that has a Scale slider to scale it; **drag a circle** (`.cmSlideHandle`) on a group fixed along an edge by a slider to slide it.
- **Reach**: with the *Reach* button on, or **Alt** held, drag a hand or a foot and the limb bends at its joints (the Turn sliders of the grabbed group and the groups above it, at most four) to bring that point to the pointer, within each slider's range; a target out of reach leaves the limb stretched toward it.
- **Escape** in the middle of a drag puts everything back; *Reset pose* puts the sliders at rest. The pointer is only a shortcut: every pose is reachable with the Ranges tab's sliders.

## Limits

| What | Warns above | Stops at |
|---|---|---|
| Points in a shape | 24 | 60 |
| Objects in a group | 24 | 48 |
| Shapes in an object | 36 | 72 |
| A character's copy of a model | 150 KB | 250 KB (Publish refused) |
| Groups in a pivot chain | 6 (the most) | above 6 (a move that makes a longer chain is refused) |
| Sliders in a model | 40 | 80 |
| Drives of one slider | 12 | 24 |
| Point links in a model | 100 | 200 (Publish refused) |
| Axes of forms in an object | 4 | 8 |
| Forms in an axis | 24 | 48 |
| Presets in a model | 40 | 80 |

The frontend numbers (`engine/limits.ts`) and the backend's (`schema.py`) are held equal by `schemaParity.test.ts`.

## Saving and publishing

Two layers, as in the Plot presets: every edit goes to the **draft** through the shared autosave (off by default; the Save button and its toggle are in the page's head), and **Publish** copies the draft to the published copy, refusing a draft that is not valid (no name, a name another published model has, a shape of fewer than three points, an object or group that points at something missing, a shape in two objects, a pivot chain deeper than six, an anchor whose shape is missing or not in the parent or above it, an anchor that slides on a slider that is not there, a slider that drives something that is not there, recorded point moves for points a shape no longer has, a twin of something that is missing, of a twin or of itself, a slider that drives a twin, a link to a shape, point or edge that is not there or to its own shape or round in a circle, a looseness slider that is not there, a back outline of fewer than three points, more than 200 point links, an axis whose default form is missing, a form that changes a shape that is not in its object or a twin or has an outline of one or two points, a preset that sets a slider or hides a thing that is not there, a copy over the size limit). **Discard changes** returns the draft to the published copy; a model never published is cleared. New versions change nothing in characters that already use a model: a character will keep its own copy.

## Tokens

Surfaces: `surface-raised`, `surface-deep`, `hairline`; text: `ink`, `ink-muted`, `ink-faint`; the Admin accent for the selection, the active row and the primary buttons (ink `on-accent`); `alert` for the danger ring and a refused action. Shapes are drawn in `ink-muted` (the selection in `accent`) until the model has colour slots. All text is at least 11px, and every pair is already in the contrast audit.

## Don't

- Don't type coordinates or amounts: a point is dragged or nudged with the arrow keys, a pivot too, and every number of a slider has a minus and a plus.
- Don't save a test value: the Try it sliders, the form chosen on each axis and the eye are for looking (a preset is how a set of them is kept).
- Don't give a form its own anchors or links: they always read the base outline.
- Don't edit a twin: edit its original, or break the pair.
- Don't drag a linked point: unlink it, or adjust the link with the steppers or the arrow keys.
- Don't draw a colour into a shape: colour comes later, from the character's palette.
- Don't hide a fault: Publish stays disabled, with the reason, until the draft is valid.
- Don't let a new version of a model change characters that already use it.
