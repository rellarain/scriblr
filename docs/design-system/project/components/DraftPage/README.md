# Draft page, preview and reactions

At Max the Draft level is the open chapter on **paper**: a light sheet with dark text by day and a dark sheet with light text at night. The right page is three tiles on the [split grid](../SplitTiles/README.md): the **chapter**, its **plotpoints** and the **draft**; the arcs' and chapters' [edge tabs](../BookCover/README.md) stand at its right.

Code: `writer/draft/DraftLevel.tsx`, `ChapterTile.tsx`, `ChapterPoints.tsx`, `DraftCards.tsx`, `PreviewPane.tsx`; classes `.wrSpread*`, `.wrChapterTile`, `.wrPreview*`, `.wrReactBar`.

## Anatomy

- **Chapter tile** `.wrChapterTile`: the titles above the chapter as tags (series, book, arc, each in its own colour with a 6px stripe; a book with no colour of its own shows its series'), then the chapter's number badge (the one the Outline's cards have, *Chapter 1* to a screen reader) over the title (serif 22 bold, click to edit) and synopsis, the dates and draft stats, and the tools at the right: Save, the **Draft | Preview** switch, Settings and Help. (**Publish** and the *Published* date are held back for WUI v2, with publishing to readers: `features.ts`.)
- **Plotpoints tile**: footnote cards of the placements in this chapter (a plotpoint placed twice here has two, each with the awareness eye of its own place), *By time* or *In story*.
- **Draft tile**: the acts, scenes and moments as cards (fold up, remembered), each around an auto-growing serif input (16px, line 1.6) with a live word count, the **paragraph tools** and the **style aids** (below). Settings (the chapter's colour and the style aids) and Help open as tiles beside it.
- **Left page and crease**: a bare strip of paper under the other levels, and an 18px crease.

## Paragraph tools and style aids

`draft/MomentDraft.tsx`, `draft/useParagraphTools.ts`, `paragraphs.ts`, `styleAids.ts`; classes `.wrMomentDraft`, `.wrPara*`, `.wrDraftMirror`, `.wrAid*`.

- **Gutter**: a 30px column left of each moment's text with a number for each paragraph (`¶1`, bold small, the paper's muted ink) at the paragraph's first line, and a pushpin beside it when a plotpoint is pinned there.
- **Tools**: the paragraph the caret is in shows, in place of its number, a column of five 26px square buttons (the paper's frame with its ink, ringed by its line; hover rings in the ink; pressed is the ink with the frame's colour; disabled at 60%): **Move up**, **Move down**, **Move to another moment** (opens the dialog), **Split at the cursor** (only with the caret inside the paragraph's words), **Join with the next**. A tool keeps the caret in the text and puts it where the paragraph went. The gutter is as tall as the tools while they are out.
- **The line under the text**, for the caret's paragraph: `¶N`, its long sentences (*Long sentence: 38 words*), each plotpoint pinned to it (*Plotpoint: The unopened letter · moves with this paragraph*, ringed by the line, with an x to unpin) and **Pin a plotpoint here…**, a select of the moment's placements.
- **Move dialog**: over the text beside the tools (the frame, ringed by the line, with `shadow-save-bubble`): *Move paragraph N to*, a chapter of the book (this one first), the chapter's moments as rows (*Scene 1 · Moment 2* in bold, then its title or synopsis; the chosen row is ringed in the ink with a 3px bar), **At the start** / **At the end**, *Its pinned plotpoint goes with it.*, **Cancel** and **Move** (the ink with the frame's colour). Escape closes it.
- **What goes with a paragraph**: a placement pinned to it, the chapter's footnotes (counted across the chapter), the Preview's reactions and the revision flags on its text (the backend finds them again). Text and plot are saved as any edit is (Save, or autosave); footnotes at once.
- **Style aids**: a copy of the text behind the transparent text box (`.wrDraftMirror`, the same box, font and line height, the text transparent) carries the marks: a **long sentence** underlined with a 1.5px wave in the chapter's accent, a **repeated word** with a 2px bar under it in the accent. Each moment's **reading level** (*Reading level: grade 7*, ringed by the line) stands at the right of its title row. In the Settings tile, under the chapter's colour and a line, **Style aids**: three switches, each with what it does (*Underline a sentence longer than [30] words.*, *Mark a word used again within [40] words. Small words (the, and, of) are left out.*, *Show each moment's reading level beside its title (for English text).*), and *Each aid is kept on this machine, for every chapter.* All are off at first.

## The preview

The chapter laid out as a reader sees it, in the book's own **preview formatting** (font, size, bold or italic, alignment, line spacing, indent, paragraph spacing), on a day or night page of its own. A toolbar sets the tool (and, in WUI v2, the version: the current draft or a published copy): **Reaction**, **Flag**, **Footnote**, **Export**.

The **Flag** tool (`draft/FlagTool.tsx`, `useRevisionFlags.ts`) keeps the project's **revision flags**, things to come back to: a **kind** (a segmented choice of seven: **Review**, **Add**, **Remove**, **Merge**, **Change**, **Simplify** and **Expand**, with what the chosen one asks beside it, such as *Say it more plainly.*), a note, and one of two icon buttons: **Flag this sentence** (disabled until a sentence is clicked; the flag is the sentence's characters in its moment, `sentenceRanges` in `sentences.ts`, and follows the text as it is edited) or **Flag the project** (not tied to a passage). A **Merge** can name a second sentence of the chapter to merge the first with: **Pick on the page** (pressed while picking) makes the next click on the page pick it instead of selecting, the picked one shows in the chapter's highlight with a dashed outline in its accent, and a cross clears it; it is optional. A flagged sentence is **underlined in the accent** while the tool is open, the second passage of a merge too. Flags made before version 1 were Edit and Delete: they read as Change and Remove. Under the toolbar each open flag of the chapter and of the project is a row: its kind (a select), its text in quotation marks (a merge's two passages joined by a plus; or "The project", or "Text gone" when the passage was cut), its note (saved when the field is left), **Show it in the text** (eye; a merge has a second eye for its other passage), **Resolve** (check: it asks what was done, Enter to confirm, Escape to cancel) and **Delete** (trash). The **Show resolved** switch brings back the resolved ones, dimmed, with what was done. The flags are read again each time the tool opens.

The **Export** tool (`draft/ExportTool.tsx`, `api/export.ts`) makes a file of **the project**, **this book** or **this chapter**, as a **PDF** (to print or share, as the preview lays it out), an **EPUB** (for e-readers) or **JSON** (the outline, plot and drafts, as data). Each choice is a row of **cards** (`.wrExportCard`: the name in bold over what it holds, such as *Book 1 · The Lantern Road · 14 chapters*), ringed by the page's line; the chosen card is the chapter's selected highlight with a 2px ring in the page ink, a hovered one the chapter's highlight. Under them, in a box ringed by the page's line, only the chosen format's **options**, as drawn tick boxes (`.wrExportBox`: a ring in the page ink; ticked, filled with the ink and the tick in the page's colour): for a PDF the **page size** (6 × 9 in, A5, US Letter, A4), **Title page**, **Contents**, **Footnotes** and **Revision flags in the margin** (each open flag beside the line its sentence starts on, its kind in bold and the start of its note, in the book's hue); for an EPUB a **Cover** (the book's title and colour), **Contents** and **Footnotes** (at each chapter's end); for JSON **Plot**, **Revision flags** and **Chapter revisions** (the outline and drafts are always in). A chapter alone has no Contents choice. A project of several books, in PDF or EPUB, chooses between **A file per book** (a zip) and **One file with every book**. Last comes the **file name** (monospace, made from the titles: `the-lantern-road-chapter-3.pdf`) and **Export** (the accent button, *Exporting…* while it runs); a failure shows as an alert under it. The choices are remembered.

On the page, a **small button** is the page ink on the chapter's highlight (the hovered one on its selected highlight), and a pressed or main one (Day or Night, Export, Flag this sentence) the accent with its own ink; a **select** is the page ink on the chapter's highlight, its options the page ink on the page. A tile's own rule (the level's wash and white text) does not reach the page, which the night and the day tone both need.

The **Footnote** tool is the author's side of a reader's margin notes: select a sentence, give the footnote a title and text, and it is kept on that sentence (a small numbered marker shows on it); a footnote can be deleted. In WUI v2 (with publishing, `features.ts`) a footnote can be unpublished, and the same tool flags a passage with one of the book's content flags (set in the Book level's Publish tab). Published footnotes and flagged passages are frozen into the next edition of the book; readers see the footnotes in the right margin of the e-reader's page and the flags by the class each reader chose.

## Reactions

Click a sentence, then the bar in the left margin. A reaction has three levels and a fourth click clears it; one glyph per level ([iconography](../../iconography.md)). Likes and dislikes are separate bars of 20px at the sentence's own height. The bar's fill strengthens with the level (saturation 30, 62, 96 / 26, 58, 92) in the accent's hue for a like and the alert's for a dislike; level 0 is a dashed outline with a `+` or `-`.

## Tokens

`paper`, `paper-soft`, `page`, `frame-1..5`, `paper-ink`, `paper-ink2`, `paper-label`, `paper-muted`, `paper-placeholder`, `paper-error`, `paper-like`, `paper-dislike`, `like-bar-*`, `dislike-bar-*`.

## Don't

- Don't use a fixed black or white on the paper: take the paper inks, which are fitted.
- Don't stack hearts to show a level; one glyph per level.
- Don't style the preview text outside the book's preview formatting.
