# Content voice

The words in the app are part of the design. They are short, plain and in the writer's own terms. This page is the one place the app's real microcopy is quoted (the previews use lorem ipsum for what is inside them; these are the labels around it).

## Rules

1. **Name things the way a writer does.** Series, book, arc, chapter, act, scene, moment, plotpoint, plotline, draft, preview, publish. Never a system's word (node, entity, record, payload).
2. **A control says what it does**, as a verb, and the result is visible: *Write*, *Publish*, *Save*, *Add chapter*, *New chapter*, *Delete*, *Confirm*, *Cancel*. A toggle names what it opens: *Book outline*, *Book details*, *Settings*, *Help*.
3. **An empty area says what is missing and the one next step.** One or two short sentences, ending with a full stop.
4. **Counts are plain and exact**: "5 chapters · 47 words", "231 words of 3,000", "1 of 3", "2 plotlines · 2 waiting". Singular and plural are right ("1 plotline"). The separator is a middle dot.
5. **Errors say what went wrong and what to do**, without apology: "Save failed." followed by the reason the system gave. The state of a save is a word, not an icon alone.
6. **A confirmation repeats what will happen**: "Delete Chapter 1 and everything in it?" then *Confirm* / *Cancel*.
7. **Sentence case for text, uppercase only for small labels** (a section mark such as CONTENTS, DRAFT STATS, PLOTPOINTS). No exclamation marks, no emoji, no jokes.
8. **Keyboard help is a hint, in full sentences**: "Enter adds a note, Shift+Enter a new line. Tab moves between notes."

## Names that never change

The levels are **Dash**, **Project**, **Outline** and **Draft**; their sizes are **Min**, **Mid** and **Max**; the zones are **Dawn**, **Day**, **Dusk** and **Night**; a user's four colours are **theme**, **accent**, **alert** and **admin accent**. The two views of a chapter are **Draft** and **Preview**; the preview's tools are **Reaction**, **Flag** and **Export**.
