# Seed model generator

Builds **Adult (basic)**, the model that comes with Scriblr, and writes it to `backend/app/seed/basic_model.json` (which the server publishes on a fresh install and offers with
*Add the basic model*). Nothing here is shipped: only the JSON it writes is.

```bash
npx vite-node scripts/seed-model/run.ts [preview-dir] [seed-file]   # build, check, write the JSON and a preview page
npx vite-node scripts/seed-model/check-fit.ts 0.08                  # how well the old outlines are fitted again at a tolerance
```

- `exact.ts` reads the old portrait engine's exact shapes (`docs/mannequin-mockup/exact-default.js`) and the mockup's own model (`designer-engine.js`).
- `outline.ts` + `fit.ts` fit the many sampled points of an old outline with a few curve points (Schneider's algorithm), within a tolerance, and tell mirror images.
- `face.ts` makes the head, ears, brows, eyes, nose and mouth: one object each, one form per option, a twin object for each pair.
- `hair.ts` makes the hair (rebuilt in phase O7 after the old portrait's hair): the mockup's fourteen sections (plane x side), each a piece drawn as an offset of the old portrait's skull (`sk`, `halfAt`: the superellipse at 54/92 of its size) ending at its default hairline (`HAIRLINE`), with a cap under it (`hc_*`) that covers its part of the scalp, a Length slider (that flares the sides and the back as they grow, `grow`) and a Texture slider (that also makes the piece stand out, `volume`), the cuts made from the piece by moving its ends (`cut`: the same points on every cut, so the sliders that move points move them on all of them), and fourteen hairstyles as presets that say where the ends of each plane come to (`STYLES`, `lengthTo`). `build.ts` also makes the beard and the moustache from the old engine's facial hair options (`docs/mannequin-mockup/export-exact.ts` exports them: run it again, then copy `exact-default.js` over the one in `docs/mannequin-mockup/`, when the old drawing's shapes change) and the *Facial hair* presets.
- `build.ts` also builds the fantasy parts (ears, eye styles, extra eyes, horns, crowns, wreaths, hoods, necklaces) from the options `export-exact.ts` writes for them; `face.ts` lays the options of more than one axis on the same channels (`also`) and gives each fantasy part its colour slot.
- `items.ts` builds the held items (phase 6c-3b): `prepareItems` takes the old drawing's items without their hand and sleeve, puts every item's grip at the middle of the mannequin's palm, draws them a quarter larger (and no more than 34 units above the hand), adds the band of skin for the fingers and the unrolled scroll; `itemSpec`/`mirrorItem` make one object for each hand (the right is the left's mirror image with an axis of its own); `itemParts` hangs each in a group under its hand, with an *Item angle* slider, a grip point and the Pose presets; `poseArmsApart` gives each arm slider (raise, elbow, wrist) a slider for each side.
- `cloth.ts` (phase O6) shades the clothes and makes them fold and hang: a shadow down each garment's far side and the inside of each sleeve and trouser leg (shading shapes), folds at the elbows, knees and waist that fade in with their sliders and folds down the skirt, the dress and the coat (ribbons made with the engine's `taperOutline`, every point following the skin under it), the right-hand pieces mirrored with `mirrored` from clothes.ts, and the weights and drape of the skirt, the dress and the coat (`clothShading`, called from build.ts after `finishClothes`).
- `clothes.ts` makes the clothes: the shirt, sleeves, trousers and shoes as offsets of the skins (every point following the skin, with its thickness), the fifteen necklines as hole forms and what each brings, and a jacket, a coat, a skirt, a dress and underwear, carried over from the mockup's body to this one (`remapNeck`, `remapBody`); the right-hand pieces are mirrored once the body has its twins (`finishClothes`).
- `body.ts` draws the realistic body (phase O4b): the proportions of a figure seven and a half heads tall (`HEAD`, `CROWN_Y`, `LANDMARKS`), one skin per segment and the joints (`JOINTS`), the skin weights given with Auto (`SKIN_WEIGHTS`, only between neighbouring segments), the one-light shading (every point following the skin under it), the sliders, the depth and per-side sliders and the bones, and the hands (phase O5: `PALM`, `FINGERS`, `THUMB`, each finger one shape over two joints made with the engine's `digitOutline`, `HAND_WEIGHTS`, the Curl that folds a finger away from the viewer with Depth drives, `HAND_SIDES` and the starter hand poses; build.ts cuts each palm with the sleeves of its own arm, `palmsUnderSleeves`). Only the head, the face and the hair follow the old portrait; `outline.ts` puts the head at the new chin (`CHIN_Y`; the mockup's chin was at 62). `presets.ts` holds the ages, builds, expressions and poses; `build.ts` puts it all together.
- `run.ts` reports how many heads tall the figure stands and each landmark against the canon (off by more than 2 units is flagged), reports the publish faults, writes the JSON, and `preview.tsx` writes a page that draws every option next to the old portrait.

The model has to stay under the size limit of a copy (2048 KB, counted by the server, with every default written out); `backend/tests/test_character_models_basic.py` and `src/assets/Interfaces/admin/characters/seedModel.test.ts` check it.
- `convert-preview.tsx` (`npx vite-node scripts/seed-model/convert-preview.tsx [dir]`) writes a page with twenty-four old portraits (hand-picked cases and random ones) next to what Convert makes of them on the basic model, with the lines the preview would list: a look at the mapping in `figureConvert.ts` after a change to the model or the conversion.
